03 / DISTINCT PRODUCTSUpper bound

A bound toward Erdős’s distinct-product problem.

  • Number theory
  • Combinatorics
  • Written proof with internal re-derivation of the main combinatorial and sieve steps.

Among integers from 1 to n, primes form a set in which every choice of three numbers has a different product. Erdős asked how large such a set can be. The upper bound here comes within a logarithmic factor of his proposed allowance beyond the prime count.

08 / RECURRING PRIME GAPSSelection theorem

The common divisor of recurring prime gaps is 2, 4 or 6.

  • Number theory
  • Written four-point selection law and gcd classification, internally reviewed using Merikoski’s corrected four-group sieve and a fixed CRT construction. The modular corollary also uses the finite-group structure theorem.

Polignac’s conjecture predicts that every positive even distance occurs infinitely often between consecutive primes. Using Merikoski’s four-group sieve, the argument constrains the greatest common divisor of the distances that recur, through a more general selection law.
