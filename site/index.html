01 / CONVOLUTIONStability theorem

When does combining two draws leave a distribution almost unchanged?

Multiplying two independent draws from the uniform distribution on a finite subgroup gives the same distribution back. A stability theorem bounds how close a probability law must be to a subgroup law when this property holds approximately.

04 / FINITE SYMMETRYStructure theorem

A seven-part pattern forced by symmetry.

In a finite abelian group of order coprime to six, join points using a symmetric set of allowed differences that includes zero. If fewer than 4/9 of the group elements are allowed and every four points contain a connected pair, the graph splits into seven classes. Each class is connected internally and to both its neighbors around a cycle.

Seven groups.

In the abelian groups covered by the theorem, the seven classes are the cosets of one subgroup.

The cycle shows the required links between classes. Additional links may occur.

06 / GEOMETRY & LEARNINGRecovery theorem

Accurate predictions recover hidden geometry.

For a fixed number of separated boundaries with bounded offsets, small Gaussian prediction error controls the error in the coefficients that describe those boundaries. The predictor must be a normalized polynomial of degree at most the number of boundaries. The boundaries can also be estimated when a small fraction of entire observations are corrupted.

Prediction error controls coefficient error

For fixed boundary count, separation and offset bound, the constant is independent of the input dimension.

11 / BOUNDARY STABILITYMatching theorem

Matching boundaries even when they cancel.

Crossing two identical decision boundaries changes a binary answer twice, cancelling the effect. For an even number of boundaries with bounded offsets and Gaussian inputs, the less often their combined answer departs from a constant, the more closely they can be matched in pairs.

Error in cancellation controls error in matching

A linear guarantee through boundary collisions, independent of the surrounding dimension.
