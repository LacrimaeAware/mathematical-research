03 / FACTORIZATIONExplicit construction

An infinite family of factorization obstructions.

A number can fail to fit into two bounded factors while its square fits into four and its cube into six. An explicit construction shows that this failure of algebraic closure persists at every sufficiently large factor limit. The argument also counts independent families of the resulting missing elements.
